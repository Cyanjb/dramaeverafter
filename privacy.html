Privacy

What this site collects, and what it doesn’t

Short version: no accounts, no cookies, no advertising trackers, and nothing here knows who you are.

Visitor statistics

DramaEverAfter uses GoatCounter to count visits. It sets no cookies and collects nothing that identifies you personally. For each page view it records the page you landed on, the site that sent you, your browser and operating system, and your country. That is all, and it is only ever read as totals.

It also counts when someone taps a “Watch on” button, so I can see which apps and which shows people actually go on to watch. That count records the button, not the person.

There is no Google Analytics, no advertising pixel, no fingerprinting and no cross-site tracking anywhere on this site.

My List

Saved titles live in your own browser’s local storage. They are never sent anywhere, I cannot see them, and they do not follow you to another device. Clearing your browser data clears them.

The contact form

Anything typed into the form on the contact page stays in your browser. It is not transmitted or stored. If you want to reach me, email works: cyan@dramaeverafter.com. Email you send me is kept only as long as it takes to deal with it.

Links to the apps

Buttons and links that take you to ReelShort, DramaBox or any other app hand you over to that company, and their privacy policy applies from that point. Some of those links earn this site a commission, which is what pays for running the database. It never changes what you are charged, and it never decides which show gets listed or how it is described.

Fonts and hosting

The site is hosted on Netlify and loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so both receive your IP address in the ordinary course of serving a page, as any web host does.

Your rights

Because nothing collected here identifies you, there is usually no personal data of yours for me to hand over or delete. If you have emailed me and want that correspondence removed, or you want to ask what I hold, write to cyan@dramaeverafter.com and I will sort it out.

If you are an actor and want your photo or profile changed or taken down, the same address works and it gets done.

Changes

If what the site collects changes, this page changes with it. Last updated September 2026.
