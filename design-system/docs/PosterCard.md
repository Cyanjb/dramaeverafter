The 2:3 drama poster card used in every rail and grid. Blush 'No poster' fallback always renders beneath the image. `railItem` inside a Rail, `sm` for the compact size, `saved` shows the star, `aiBadge`/`soonBadge` for the corner pills.

```tsx
<Rail>
  <PosterCard railItem title="How to Tame a Silver Fox" app="ReelShort" meta="417M views · age gap" saved={false} />
</Rail>
```
