The story

She gave up a fortune for him. He had her put in prison.

Nicole walked away from her family fortune seven years ago because she wanted Ethan instead, and for a while she thought she was the happiest wife and mother in the world. Then Ethan and her best friend Elaine had her sent to jail and took her daughter away from her. She can see the pair of them clearly now, and what she wants back is her heiress status, her child, and the chance to show everyone exactly how wrong they were to betray her.
