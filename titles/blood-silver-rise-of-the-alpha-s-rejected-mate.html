The story

She left behind a severed ribbon and a secret she did not know she had.

Ann Reed is forced into a mating bond with Alpha Dane Montague after a betrayal that wrecks two packs, and Dane holds her responsible for his family's death. He torments her for three years and is determined never to love her. When he finally claims her and then shoves her aside, she cuts the soul bond and disappears. Years later she comes back as Aurora Moon, heir to a royal European pack, with wealth and power and children to protect. What she wants is to stay well away from Dane.
