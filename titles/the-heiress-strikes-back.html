The story

Her kidnappers kept her on as the maid.

Amelia was taken as a baby and has spent her life since working as a maid for the people who took her. The Kellers are her brothers and they have never once stopped looking for her. They close in on where she is, and then the wrong girl is identified as their sister and the whole search stalls again. It comes down to a DNA test to find justice for Amelia.
