The story

She married the wrong stranger. Or exactly the right one.

Emma escaped a hard country life and built herself a fresh start in the city. Then one case of mistaken identity later, she's married to a handsome stranger called William, with no clue that he's the CEO of Irving Corporation. They both want a quick divorce but the sparks start flying the moment they move in together. Now there's a company to rescue, villains circling, and two people slowly admitting this marriage was never a mistake at all.
