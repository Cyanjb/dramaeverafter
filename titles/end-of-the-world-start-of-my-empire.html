The story

The apocalypse took everything. Alien tech is how he takes it back.

After World War III, Arthur Morgan survives on scraps while his former classmates bully and rob him for sport. Then he stumbles on alien technology, and the balance of power shifts overnight. He rescues three women who become his wives, and begins his climb from scavenger to King of the Wasteland. The classmates who tormented him are about to learn some respect.
