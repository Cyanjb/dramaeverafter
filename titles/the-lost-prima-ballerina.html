The story

A shooting at a Halloween party, and a daughter gone.

Eve Wilson loses her daughter Amelia in the panic after a shooter turns a Halloween party into chaos. Thirteen years on, the real Amelia has been kidnapped and switched and is waiting tables for the woman pretending to be her mother. What changes it is a ballet competition, and where that puts her is back within reach of her biological family.
