The story

He spent eight months looking for the woman who saved his life.

Lee Kanghyun is a boss people call the most dangerous man in the world, and for eight months he hasn't stopped hunting for the woman who once saved him. When he finally tracks her down, Yoo Seoyeon is bringing up a child on her own, cut off even by her own family. Then he notices the child's birthday lines up exactly with the night they spent together. He tells her he'll take responsibility for her and the baby, and the most dangerous man in the world starts making moves to keep them both safe. He loves her, and the little one might be his.
