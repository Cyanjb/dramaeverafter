The story

A maid in the house her mother built.

Sara's late mother built that home from nothing, and now Sara scrubs its floors as the maid. The one thing she wants back is a painting her mother left her, and she finally tracks it down in the hands of Noah Dempsey, a billionaire, and the man she once stood in for as a substitute bride. Getting the painting back means walking straight back into his life and into a house of lies.
