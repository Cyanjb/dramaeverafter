The story

Betrayed into a gold mine, he woke up seeing through stone.

It is 1849 in the American West, and William is a poor dockworker sold out by his lover and his boss until he is working the mines as a slave. Close to death, he awakens the Golden Eye, which lets him see through anything and find what is hidden. From a nobody with nothing, he builds himself into a legendary mining tycoon and goes back for every elite who put him down there.
