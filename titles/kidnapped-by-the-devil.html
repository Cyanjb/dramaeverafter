The story

Her fiance gambled her in a game of Russian roulette. He lost.

The arms dealer calls it a test of love: Russian roulette, with the bride as the stake. Her fiance fails it, and the ruthless man who set the game walks away with her. Held by a devil who intends to keep her, she feels her moral lines start to blur into something dangerously close to attraction. He will stop at nothing to make her his. The frightening part is how much of her is starting to want him too.
