The story

The CEO by day and the stranger at night are the same man.

Isla's new internship comes with Christian, a cold, demanding CEO who shows her no mercy, even on the tennis court. What Isla has not worked out is that the masked man opening her eyes at a very exclusive club every night is the same Christian who gives her orders all day. When the mask finally comes off, their two worlds collide, and Isla has to decide whether this is a game she can survive, or one that will pull her under completely.
