The story

They're engaged. They just don't know it's to each other.

With a business marriage looming, Emma Watson, heiress of the Watson family, takes an internship at Anderson Corporation to size up the family she is meant to marry into. Ethan Anderson, the heir, steps in as the new CEO to do exactly the same thing. Neither admits who they are, and ironically they fall for each other at first sight anyway. Then someone else claims Emma's identity, while she mistakes another man for her Ethan, and both of them set about breaking off engagements to people they have never actually met. Neither realises the person each is trying to escape is the love standing right in front of them.
