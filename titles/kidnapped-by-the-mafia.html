Her boyfriend sold her. Her buyer fell in love.

Violet's own boyfriend sells her to the mafia king Vincenzo as his contract bride, and escape is the only thing on her mind. Instead she discovers a heart of gold behind the fearsome name, and it beats entirely for her.
