The story

Rock bottom, meet billionaire.

In one brutal stretch, Grace Esther learns she is not her mother's real daughter, gets thrown out of the house for it, and loses her boyfriend to her stepsister on the way down. The landing though is softer than she expected. An old family friend takes her in, and his grandson Aden, a very attractive CEO, makes her his assistant, with romance following close behind. And somewhere out there, Grace's real father has just learned she exists and he is determined to find her and set things right.
