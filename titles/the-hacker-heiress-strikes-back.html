The story

The fake heiress killed her once already.

In her first life Emily was a genius hacker and the real heiress, until the fake heiress Olivia accused her of plagiarism in front of the whole world. Her family believed it and threw her out, and in the end Olivia killed her. Then Emily wakes up reborn. This time she cuts ties with the family who refused to see the truth, and gives Olivia the slap in the face she deserved all along.
