The story

He is the last person she should want.

Sylvia Parker is fighting her way through a cutthroat campus and a family that gives her nothing. The one person who steadies her is Lawrence Calhoun, who is cold, strict and the last man she should want. He is also her professor. Whatever is growing between them would destroy both of them if anyone found out.
