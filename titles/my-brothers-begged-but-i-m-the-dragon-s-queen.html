The story

Her brothers offered her to the dragon. The dragon made her a queen.

The Frostmark Empire is trapped in eternal winter, and only a marriage between a pure blooded princess and the Abyss Dragon can bring back the sun. Diana grew up adored by her three brothers, until Lorelei arrived with a stolen dragon's scale and stole her place in every heart that mattered. The brothers cast Diana aside and handed her to the dragon as a sacrifice. Only once she was gone did the truth come out, and by then their begging meant nothing. Diana belonged to the dragon now, and their apologies came too late.
