The story

She thanked the wrong man and it cost her everything.

In her first life Grace Adler mistook who had saved her, and the Sixth Prince Xavier Windsor and his concubine Lillian Bennett used that mistake to frame her and destroy her family. She comes back with every memory intact. This time she keeps Xavier at arm's length, stays ahead of Lillian, and puts her weight behind the deposed Crown Prince Roderick Windsor instead.
