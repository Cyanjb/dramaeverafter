The story

Her brother bullies the same girl that he has spent years grieving.

Stella Hearth was kidnapped as a child while her brother Dennis watched, helpless to stop it. Years later she is saved by a poor man who takes a janitor's job at her school, and the life she returns to looks nothing like the one she lost. Dennis does not recognise the sister standing right in front of him, so he bullies her along with everyone else. The day he realises who she is will break him.
