The story

He has to tutor the boy he most despises.

Chris Blanning tops the year at a private school full of snobs, which buys him bullies rather than friends, and his scholarship does not stretch far enough to cover the fees. So he has to take the tutoring work, and the pupil is Lucien Alaric, the spoiled troublemaker he watched trying to charm a teacher into changing his grades. Worse, Lucien has worked out the secret Chris has kept from everyone. Keep your enemies close, then. Chris may be keeping this one closer than he means to.
