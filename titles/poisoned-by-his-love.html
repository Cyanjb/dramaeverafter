The story

Her sister ran from the mafia king's altar. Lily has to stand there instead.

Lily Watson is a nurse, twenty five, and today her half sister Victoria is getting married. The groom is a ruthless mafia king named Dominic Castellano, and the story going around is that he killed the last woman he married. Then Victoria runs. Dominic's hitmen turn their threats on Lily's family instead. To keep the people she loves alive, Lily walks down the aisle in her sister's place. She says her vows to a man she's afraid could be a murderer, and now she has to live as his wife.
