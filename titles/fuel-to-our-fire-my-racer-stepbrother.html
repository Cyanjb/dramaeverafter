The story

The champion racer is her stepbrother. She finds out too late.

Elaine dances at illegal street races to pay the bills that support her ailing mother and her abusive stepfather. One night she meets the most feared street racer in the world, not knowing who he is she gives him her virginity. But a shock from soap opera hell is waiting for her because she's about to find out he's her stepbrother.
