The story

Her new bodyguard is the fiance who faked the affair.

Elena and Ace were engaged, until the war left Ace disabled and he faked an affair to set her free from what he had become. Three years later a shooting leaves Elena needing a personal bodyguard, and the man who takes the job is Ace. Guarding her means facing everything he walked away from, and whatever is left of the love they never actually lost.
