The story

He's earning affection points from a zombie girl.

The apocalypse turns ninety percent of humanity into zombies, and Oliver Burton accidentally activates the Romance System. It does not limit itself to humans. Oliver ends up bound to Emily, a beautiful zombie girl, and earns her affection point by point with offerings of raw meat. Then his treacherous neighbors Jack and Katie come demanding food, and when he refuses they start plotting a raid. Oliver is already one step ahead.
