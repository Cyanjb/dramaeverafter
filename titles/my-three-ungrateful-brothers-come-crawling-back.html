The story

She hid her fortune to protect their pride. They threw her away anyway.

Oriana lost her mother at six and got dumped at a trailer park by her stepmother. At twelve she saved Murray Morel, the richest man on earth, and became his adopted granddaughter. When her three blood brothers finally found her, she went home to them, hiding her wealth to spare their egos while secretly helping them succeed. Then their scheming stepsister turned the brothers against her, and they beat her half to death and threw her out. Now Oriana is done protecting them, and she wants everything back.
