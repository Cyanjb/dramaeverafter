The story

The weakest Love System, bound to the strongest zombie.

Ethan is a mercenary on the apocalypse battlefield when he awakens the weakest Love System ever recorded. The target it binds him to is Emily, an SSS rank mutant zombie. A handshake earns fresh meat, a first kiss grants a vaccine, and a bond unlocks a genetic evolution serum. Everyone else upgrades one ability at a time. Ethan draws every resource the wasteland has by taming her, and as her affection climbs the savage beast bows to him, with a gaze that grows more dangerous the closer she gets.
