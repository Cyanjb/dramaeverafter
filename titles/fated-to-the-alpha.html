The story

She runs from her own pack and finds him.

Katya has not come into her wolf yet, but she is a Luna healer, and the only one who can heal the girl who has spent years bullying her: her own Alpha's daughter. The title brings threats with it, so Katya runs for the Black Creek pack. There she meets Alpha Ezra, who turns out to be her fated wolf and the one man willing to stand in front of her.
