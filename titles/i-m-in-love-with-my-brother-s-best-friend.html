The story

She moves in with her boyfriend and catches him cheating the same day.

Kaitlyn Sinclair starts her freshman year sharing an off campus apartment with her high school sweetheart, and catches him cheating almost immediately. With nowhere else to go she moves in with her brother, which means sharing the place with his best friend Cole, who is doing grad school on the same campus. The crush she had on him as a child blooms once again. Between spiteful exes, mean girls and her own brother, there is no shortage of people who would like to put distance between Cole and Kaitlyn. Will they succeed?
