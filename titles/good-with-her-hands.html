The story

Her first love walks into her office needing help.

Bella and Adam fell in love at first sight in college and were pulled apart before it could go anywhere. Years later Bella is a successful doctor, and the patient who walks into her office is Adam, her first love, now an NHL superstar in need of help. A twist of fate binds them in a contract relationship, and their unfinished love gets its second chance. The spark is still there. What they have to decide is whether it can last this time.
