The story

The baby isn't her husband's.

Luciana Bridge is about to tell her husband the insemination worked when she catches him cheating. Then the clinic calls about a mix up, and the baby she is carrying belongs to the powerful billionaire David Carlson. One arrangement later she is living in his mansion as his secret surrogate and his very public fiancee. But David wants far more from Luciana than her surrogacy services, and he is not a man who gives up.
