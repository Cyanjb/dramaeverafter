The story

Her parents sold her. She ran before the deal closed.

Riley Jones flees her forced marriage on the wedding day itself, after her groom Corbin Hicks tries to force himself on her. The stranger who saves her is Xander Thorne. Only later does Riley learn the whole truth: her parents really were selling her, and the stranger who caught her is far more than he seems. Some rescues are fate.
