The story

He swore a blood oath to keep her safe, then missed the night it mattered.

Scarlett was an orphan on the streets when the Vampire Lord Sean took her in as his thrall and his lover and swore a blood oath to protect her. Then Chelsea arrives and takes him. Sean turns Chelsea into a vampire, lets her drink from Scarlett as well, and is distracted enough to miss the night of the oath entirely, which leaves Scarlett dying. She chooses herself instead and risks everything to sever the bond. The one who saves her is Vampire Prince Alder, brought back together after she once helped him. Is this fate?
