# CandyJar's two IMDb company entities — corrected reading (2026-08-10)

My earlier note in imdb_candyjar_2026-08-09.json called Inkitt Media (co1110145)
the PARENT and a SUPERSET of Candy Jar (co1130595). That was too strong, and
Beastly Lights disproves it.

Observed across 13 CandyJar title pages in this batch:

  BOTH co1110145 + co1130595 : Grayson, Luna Graced, French Kiss,
                               Conflict of Interest, Exercise Discretion
  co1110145 (Inkitt) ONLY    : Don't Say Te Amo, Spoiled Rotten,
                               The Ecstasy of Faking It, Next Door,
                               Secrets of Siren, The Tutors
  co1130595 (Candy Jar) ONLY : Beastly Lights
  NEITHER                    : My Silent Treasure (Purplelight Pictures)

CONCLUSION: neither entity is a superset of the other. The catalogue is split
across both, with a majority-but-not-all overlap, and at least one title sits
under a third company entirely.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE OPEN QUESTION. READ FIRST asks where the 11 CandyJar titles
absent from co1130595's page live. The answer is probably co1110145, since six
titles here carry that credit alone — but pulling co1110145 will NOT by itself
give the whole catalogue either. BOTH company pages need reading, and the union
still misses anything credited to an outside production house like Purplelight.

Do not treat a single company page as the archive. co1130595 was already
described as "a floor, not the archive"; this shows co1110145 is the same.
